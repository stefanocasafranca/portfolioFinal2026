I'm Strategic Planner, Tech Initiatives at the Center for Government and Civic Service in Austin, a role I've held since April 2025. In practice it's an AI engineering role.

I built a production agent with Python and LangGraph that parses intake requests, checks availability, and drafts confirmations, using LangGraph state management for short-term and long-term memory across a multi-step workflow. I designed and implemented the evaluation framework that decides whether it ships: task-level success criteria, guardrails, and a human review gate, with every decision traced in LangSmith for observability.

A lot of that job is not code. I ran the technical assessment and requirements work directly with the staff who own the process, translating what they described into a specification and presenting recommendations back to them.

I also explained agent orchestration and AI evaluation to a 16-person cohort of developers and non-technical staff, designing the full curriculum myself, and shipped MCP tool integrations deployed via CI/CD on GitHub Actions. That cohort is the Public Service Software Factory, a pilot I run that embeds AI-assisted development into an internship scrum model for non-technical teams, with students building public-service AI solutions using Claude Code, LangChain, and common web stacks.

Beyond the engineering work I've driven cross-functional initiatives at the center, including a 170-participant hackathon in partnership with NASA Space Apps that secured more than 25,000 dollars in sponsorships, and the end-to-end rollout of a new website built with Astro and TailwindCSS.

Before this I was a Design Engineer at the ACC Bioscience Incubator from July 2024 to February 2025, where I ran discovery with incubator leadership and rebuilt their site end to end, cutting key task-completion time by 60 percent and presenting the findings in the incubator's first annual report.

I also coordinated the Food Access Program at ACC Social Support Resource Development from March to June 2025, running guerrilla UX research on navigation and access barriers in food-access wayfinding and supporting service delivery for more than 250 households. I've worked as a UX and Product Designer at Redivo.app since December 2025.
