I'm Stefano Casafranca Laos. I'm an AI engineer at the Center for Government and Civic Service in Austin, Texas, where my title is Strategic Planner, Tech Initiatives. I build production agent systems, and I came to them through design research rather than through a computer science pipeline.

I'm originally from Lima, Peru, and I'm bilingual in English and Spanish.

My educational background combines design and engineering. I'm completing an A.A.S. in Software Development and Computer Science at Austin Community College, graduating May 2026. Before that I earned a B.A. in Industrial Design with a product design focus from Pontifical Catholic University of Peru, studying from 2018 to 2023.

That combination is the whole point of how I work. I can build an agent with LangGraph and evaluate it properly, and I can also run the user research that tells me whether it should exist at all. Most people in this field have one of those, not both.
